📋 Bulk import

Paste a list of series or book titles, one per line (e.g. exported from Goodreads/StoryGraph, or just typed out). Each line will be searched on Audible and matched to a series — you'll get a chance to review and pick which ones to subscribe to before anything happens. Limited to 60 lines per import, and searches are paced about a second apart to stay polite to Audible — a full batch can take a minute or two.
